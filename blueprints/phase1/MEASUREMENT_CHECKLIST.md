# Phase 1 Measure + Photo Checklist (P1-T-002)

For: Shane, in the wrestling room with a tape and your phone.
Write everything in feet-inches (example: 32'-6"). If you can't get something, write "skip" and move on.
Tip: sketch a box for each room on paper, mark north if you know it, and put the numbers on the sketch. Then snap a photo of the sketch.

## A. Wrestling room
1. Length, wall to wall (long way).
2. Width, wall to wall (short way).
3. Ceiling height (floor to ceiling, or to the bottom of beams if it's open).
4. Each door: which wall, distance from the nearest corner, door width, and which way it swings (in/out, hinge left/right).
5. Each window: which wall, width, height, and height of the sill off the floor.
6. Anything on the walls that pads would cover: outlets, light switches, thermostats, fire alarms, fire extinguishers, vents. For each: which wall, distance from the corner, height off the floor.
7. Wall material (block, drywall, metal, brick, other). Knock on it.
8. Existing wall pads, if any: which walls, height off the floor to the top of the pad, thickness, and condition.

## B. Walk-through room (route to the training room)
9. Length and width.
10. Ceiling height.
11. Layout: where every door is and which rooms they lead to (wrestling room, training room, hallway, outside).

## C. Restrooms + plumbing
12. Existing restroom fixtures anywhere nearby: count the toilets, urinals, sinks, showers, and floor drains, and note where each one is.
13. Where leaks or water damage show: stains, wet spots, soft floor, rust, mold. Which room and which wall.
14. Where water lines or drain pipes show: exposed pipes, cleanouts, shutoff valves, floor drains.

## D. Photos (straight on, room lights on)
15. Each wall of the wrestling room, straight on (4 photos).
16. Each corner of the wrestling room.
17. The ceiling.
18. Each wall of the walk-through room, plus each of its doors.
19. Every existing restroom fixture.
20. Every leak or water-damage spot, close up, with something in the shot for scale (tape, a hand, a shoe).
21. Any exposed pipes, valves, or drains.
22. Your paper sketch with the numbers on it.

Send photos + numbers back however is easiest. KEYSTONE turns them into the existing-conditions plan (P1-A-101).
