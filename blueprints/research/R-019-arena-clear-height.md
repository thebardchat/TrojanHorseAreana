# R-019 — Arena clear height for basketball, volleyball and wrestling (P2-A-201 elevations)

Researched by KEYSTONE 2026-10-04 for P2-A-201 Rev A (exterior elevations). All sources retrieved 2026-10-04.
Status: research note. These are recommendations from rule-book specifications and planning standards, not building code.

## Sources
- **S1** — Draper Inc., "High School Basketball and Volleyball Gymnasium Equipment Court Design & Rules" (reprints the NFHS court specifications) — https://www.draperinc.com/documentdownload.aspx?file=Gym_Court_Design_Rules-HS_0916.pdf&path=images%2FCatalogs%2FWhitePapers — SECONDARY (manufacturer reprint; the NFHS rule book itself is paywalled, not reviewed).
- **S2** — City of Los Angeles Recreation and Parks, design standards checklist for gyms and community buildings (2025-05-29) — https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/planning/park-planning/pdf/2025.05.29%20RAP%20DESIGN%20STANDARDS_%20gym%20&%20community%20bldgs%20checklist.pdf — PLANNING STANDARD (one city's owner standard).
- **S3** — NFHS, "Emphasizing Student Safety When Remodeling Wrestling Facilities" — https://nfhs.org/stories/emphasizing-student-safety-when-remodeling-wrestling-facilities — NFHS article.

QUESTION: What clear height should the arena volume have?

ANSWER: S1 (basketball court specifications): "It is recommended that the area above the court be clear of any obstructions and be at least 25 feet or higher"; for volleyball, at least 23 ft. S2 lists a gym ceiling height of 25 ft clearance minimum. S3 recommends 9-10 ft clear for wrestling **practice rooms**; KEYSTONE found no NFHS clear height for competition wrestling, so basketball (25 ft) governs the multi-use floor. P2-A-201 uses **≥ 25 ft clear (cited)** and draws the underside of the arena structure at 36 ft (ASSUMED: also leaves ≈ 15 ft over an assumed 21 ft upper-tier top aisle, the IBC 1030.6.2.2 roof height for smoke-protected seating, R-018), roof top 42 ft (ASSUMED). Scoreboards, lighting, rigging and the tier section can raise this; NCAA figures were not checked.

CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM (consistent recommendations, secondary reprint of the NFHS text).

NEEDS ARCHITECT CONFIRMATION: YES (structure depth, rigging, scoreboard, sightlines, roof form).
