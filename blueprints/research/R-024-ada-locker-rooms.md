## Scoping (S1, Chapter 2)

- **213.3.1 Toilet compartments:** "Where toilet compartments are provided, at least one toilet compartment shall comply with 604.8.1." An ambulatory compartment (604.8.2) is required only where six or more compartments are provided, or where urinals plus water closets total six or more.
- **213.3.6 Bathing facilities:** "Where bathtubs or showers are provided, at least one bathtub complying with 607 or at least one shower complying with 608 shall be provided."
- **222.1 Dressing, fitting and locker rooms:** "at least 5 percent, but no fewer than one, of each type of use in each cluster provided shall comply with 803."
- **225.2.1 Lockers:** "at least 5 percent, but no fewer than one of each type, shall comply with 811."

## Technical (S1, Chapters 3-9)

**Space and doors**
- **304.3.1:** turning space 60 in diameter minimum. 304.3.2 allows a T-shape in a 60 in square instead.
- **305.3:** clear floor space 30 × 48 in minimum.
- **308.2.1:** unobstructed forward reach 15 in minimum to 48 in maximum.
- **403.5.1:** accessible route clear width 36 in minimum.
- **404.2.3:** door clear width 32 in minimum.
- **Table 404.2.4.1:** front approach, pull side: 60 in perpendicular and 18 in beyond the latch.

**Water closets and lavatories**
- **604.2:** water closet centerline 16-18 in from the side wall; 17-19 in in an ambulatory compartment.
- **604.3.1:** clearance 60 in from the side wall × 56 in from the rear wall.
- **604.8.1.1:** wheelchair compartment 60 in wide minimum × 56 in deep (wall-hung) or 59 in deep (floor-mounted).
- **604.8.1.2:** compartment door in the front partition, with the opening ≤ 4 in from the side partition farthest from the WC.
- **604.8.2.1:** ambulatory compartment 35-37 in wide × 60 in deep minimum.
- **606.2:** lavatory forward approach with knee and toe clearance (306).
- **606.3:** lavatory rim ≤ 34 in.

**Showers**
- **608.2.1:** transfer shower 36 × 36 in inside, with a 36 in entry and a clearance 36 in wide × 48 in long.
- **608.2.2:** standard roll-in shower 30 in wide × 60 in deep inside, with a 60 in entry.
- **608.2.2.1:** roll-in clearance 30 × 60 in adjacent to the open face.

**Locker rooms, storage and benches**
- **803.2:** turning space inside the locker room.
- **803.3:** doors may swing into the room only with a 30 × 48 in space beyond the swing.
- **803.4:** "A bench complying with 903 shall be provided within the room."
- **811.2 / 811.3:** storage needs clear floor space (305) and a reach range per 308.
- **903.2:** bench clear floor space at the end of the seat, parallel to its short axis.
- **903.3:** bench seat 42 in long minimum × 20-24 in deep.
- **903.4:** back support, or the bench affixed to a wall.
- **903.5:** seat height 17-19 in.

## IBC 2021 (S2)

- **1006.2.1 / Table 1006.2.1:** one exit access doorway is allowed in Group A for ≤ 49 occupants with a common path ≤ 75 ft (sprinklered). The event locker rooms have 18 occupants each (900 / 50 gross, Table 1004.5).
- **1010.1.1:** doors 32 in minimum clear.
- **1010.1.2.1:** doors swing in the direction of egress only where the room serves 50 or more occupants. Below that, the room door may swing in, which keeps the 8 ft exit passages clear.
