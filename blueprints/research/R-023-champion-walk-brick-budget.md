## QUESTION

How many engraved bricks fit the 840 SF Champion Walk, and what would the vendors charge for them under a few size mixes, with and without the $6 logo?

## ANSWER

**Counting basis.** Bricks are counted at their nominal face size: 4x8 = 32 sq in = 0.2222 SF and 8x8 = 64 sq in = 0.4444 SF. That is the D-046 basis. The full 840 SF is engraved brick, with no deduction for a border course, joints, cuts or waste. These counts are therefore an **upper bound**. Shane's own allowance for border and joints is ≈ 3,700 4x8 (S2). The border width, layout and joint size are TBD.

**Scenarios.** Vendor cost includes engraving and shipping (S1).

**NOT included, and NOT estimated here:**
- **Installation labor** (layout, setting, cutting, compaction, joint sand): TBD. For scale only, S3 publishes residential brick-walkway labor of "$8 to $16 per square foot" (includes light site prep). Applied to 840 SF that is $6,720–$13,440. This is **REFERENCE, confidence LOW**: residential, not a commercial plaza, not local, not a quote. It is not added to any total above.
- **Base / sub-base** (excavation, compacted aggregate, sand or concrete setting bed, drainage): TBD, sized by the civil / landscape designer for the site soils. S1 itself says to budget it in the Phase 2 site work. No number is given.
- **Border / edge restraint** (material and labor): TBD. Border width and material are not chosen. S3 lists residential "edging and borders" at $5–$25 per linear foot (REFERENCE, LOW). No total is given because the edge length and layout are TBD.
- **Engraving labor beyond the vendor price**: S1 says its vendor prices *include* engraving, so the standard engraving of each brick is in the table. Anything beyond that is not included and not estimated: extra lines or characters past a vendor's standard, on-site or replacement engraving, order handling and data entry, or a program administrator.
- Also not included: sales tax, contingency, permits, site lighting, sealing, maintenance, replacement bricks, and the cost of running the donor program.
