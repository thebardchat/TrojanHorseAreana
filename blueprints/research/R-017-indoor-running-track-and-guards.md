## R-017.1 Lane width and lane count

QUESTION: How wide should a recreational indoor running / walking loop be, per lane and overall?

ANSWER: S1 §4.1.7 sets an indoor running track at a minimum of 12 ft wide with at least 3 lanes of 42 in. S2 reports three lanes of 36-42 in as typically adequate for recreational tracks and cites ACSM's 20 ft inside radius for turns. KEYSTONE uses **42 in per lane** (the top of S2's range, S1's figure). With the upper tier fixed at 15 ft and the box at 204 x 252 ft, only **7 ft = 2 lanes** fits without moving the tier or growing the box; the existing Rev C east walkway was already 7 ft. **2 lanes is ASSUMED and is fewer than both guides recommend (3).** 3 lanes x 42 in = 10.5 ft (S2); 12 ft (S1). The loop has squared corners (no 20 ft radius turns), so it suits walking and easy running more than fast running.

## R-017.2 Laps per mile

ANSWER: S3: no more than 12 laps per mile, 8-10 preferred. Rev D centerline = 2 x (148 + 205) = 706 ft → 5,280 / 706 = **7.48 laps per mile**, which is fewer laps (a longer loop) than the preferred range, so it meets the guidance. Lane 1 (inside) ≈ 2 x (144.5 + 201.5) = 692 ft; lane 2 (outside) ≈ 720 ft (arithmetic, lane centerlines 1.75 ft in from each edge).

## R-017.3 Crossings

ANSWER: S3 names users crossing the track as a hazard and recommends bringing stairs up inside the track perimeter. In Rev D the stairs and the elevator open onto the outside of the loop, and on event days spectators cross it to reach the upper tier (the loop is the upper concourse on event days). KEYSTONE treats the loop as a training feature on non-event days only; crossing points, signage and lane markings are architect / operations items.

## R-017.4 Guards at open edges

ANSWER: S4 §1015.2: guards are required along open-sided walking surfaces located more than 30 in above the floor or grade below, measured within 36 in horizontally of the edge. §1015.3: guards at least 42 in high. §1015.4: openings must not pass a 4 in sphere (with listed exceptions). §1030.17: guards at assembly seating (cross aisles, perimeter of tiers) have their own heights. Rev D marks 42 in guards on the loop's west edge (over the arena / athlete corridor) and the south edge over the double-height lobby; the tier backs need rails / guards per 1030.17 (architect).
