## R-014.1 — Net-to-gross (walls, structure, circulation)

QUESTION: What gross-up factor turns net room area into building area?

ANSWER (one paragraph): **Base 1.25; lean check 1.15.** S2: "Add **25% Net to Gross Factor for walls, structure and circulation**." S1 (Loudoun 2014 rec center): program space uses "20% circulation/80% efficiency factor" (÷0.80 = ×1.25), "reduced to account [for] pool deck and gymnasium sidelines included in program area"; office/storage use 23%/77%; then the whole building adds a 7% "Building Common Functions Area Allowance" and a **15% "Gross Building Area Allowance"** (S1's total chain is ≈ ×1.54 from assignable to gross). The test-fit uses **×1.25** as the base (S2; S1's program-space step), and **×1.15** (S1's last step only) as a lean check that assumes the separately programmed concourse carries all circulation. The full S1 chain (×1.54) would make the result larger.

SOURCE: S1, S2.

EDITION / SECTION: S1 §4.3 (June 2014); S2 space-needs summary.

CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM (two independent public documents; neither is an arena).

NEEDS ARCHITECT CONFIRMATION: YES.

## R-014.2 — Mechanical space

QUESTION: How much area for mechanical rooms?

ANSWER (one paragraph): S3: "Area requirement for facility HVAC equipment is typically **5 percent of gross floor space**; however, the requirement can vary depending on space criteria and other factors." Used as 5% of gross for the two mechanical rooms on the reference plan. Electrical/IT rooms are not separately covered. A large arena volume may need more (or rooftop units, less floor area).

SOURCE: S3.

EDITION / SECTION: MIL-HDBK-1027/4A, Mechanical Room.

## R-014.3 — Room sizes used for TBD support rooms

QUESTION: What sizes for rooms the reference plan shows without SF?

ANSWER (one paragraph): From S1 (Loudoun 2014 rec center, assignable SF):
- Vestibule 200; Lobby 400; Customer Service Desk/Control 200 → used for the **foyer / trophy lobby** (sum computed by the sheet script).
- First-Aid room **120** → **first aid** (plan shows 2–3 rooms; only 1 counted).
- Recreation Center Manager Office 150; Assistant Center Manager Office 120; Office Storage/Workroom 200 → **administrative office** 470 (the mix is KEYSTONE's choice).
- Gym Storage **700** ("Includes storage for gym floor covers") → **equipment storage** (storage for four 42 ft mats is NOT separately sized).
- Equip Storage/Maintenance Room **300** → **equipment room**.
- Kitchen **275** → **concession stand** proxy (concessions scope OPEN, D-011).
- Locker Facilities "M/F Lockers-Showers-toilets" 3,600 (context only; Phase 2 locker tags are kept).
**No source found for the stage or the utility room**; both stay TBD and are left out of the totals.

**Event / visitor locker rooms (DR3, 4 rooms):** S5: high school competition has **14 weight classes**. Team size 14 wrestlers + **4 staff (ASSUMED)** = 18 × **50 gross SF/occupant** (S4, IBC Table 1004.5 "Locker rooms") = 900 SF each, 3,600 SF for 4. The IBC factor is an egress density, used here as a sizing yardstick (method ASSUMED).

**Concourse:** S4 "Standing space 5 net" × **25% of spectators on the concourse at peak (ASSUMED)** = 2,200 × 0.25 × 5 = 2,750 SF. No published arena concourse factor was found in a primary or public planning source.

SOURCE: S1, S4, S5.

EDITION / SECTION: S1 §4.3; IBC 2021 Table 1004.5; AHSAA 2026-27 item 7.

CONFIDENCE: LOW to MEDIUM per room (see P2-G-003 table). These are proxies from a different building type.
