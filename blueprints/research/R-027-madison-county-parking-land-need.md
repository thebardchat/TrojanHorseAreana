## R-027.1 — Does unincorporated Madison County set a parking minimum?

QUESTION: What off-street parking count does Madison County, Alabama require for a 2,200-seat arena on an unincorporated Hazel Green parcel?

ANSWER: **None by zoning.** S1 (county FAQ) and S6 (R-006): the county states there are **no zoning requirements** limiting construction projects or signage. There is therefore **no published county zoning table** of spaces-per-seat or spaces-per-SF for assembly / stadium / arena in unincorporated Madison County. Parking quantity is **not a county zoning entitlement to size against** until (a) the parcel (D-006) is known and (b) Public Works confirms what the Commercial Site Plan / IFC review will require (S2, S3). Building, fire, and trades codes still apply (S1).

## R-027.4 — ADA accessible spaces (when parking is provided)

QUESTION: How many accessible spaces if the lot is sized at the 550 planning figure?

ANSWER: Per S5 Table 208.2, for a facility total of **501–1,000** spaces the minimum accessible count is **2% of total**. At 550: **0.02 × 550 = 11** accessible spaces; at least **1 of every 6** accessible (or fraction of 6) must be van-accessible → **ceil(11/6) = 2 van-accessible**. Calculate separately for each parking facility if the site has more than one lot/garage. If the total provided differs from 550, recompute from Table 208.2 — do not freeze 11.

SOURCE: S5.
CONFIDENCE: HIGH (federal table).
NEEDS architect confirmation: YES (dispersion to accessible entrances).
