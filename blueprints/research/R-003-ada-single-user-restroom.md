## R-003.1 — Which rules apply to the Phase 1 restroom split?

QUESTION: Does the 2010 ADA Standards govern new single-user restrooms built in an existing Madison County public high school?

ANSWER (one paragraph): **Yes, as an alteration by a public (Title II) entity.**
- 28 CFR 35.151(b)(1): an altered part of a facility must, "to the maximum extent feasible," be made readily accessible. Work starting on or after March 15, 2012 must comply with the 2010 Standards (35.151(c)(3)).
- 2010 Standards 202.3: each altered element or space must comply with Chapter 2. 213.2: "Where toilet rooms are provided, each toilet room shall comply with 603."
- In Alabama, DCM applies the 2010 ADA Standards to K-12 school work and says they supersede the IBC/A117.1 accessibility provisions (S3).
- Exceptions to watch:
  - **213.2 Exception 4:** in a cluster of single-user toilet rooms, "no more than 50 percent of the single user toilet rooms for each use at each cluster" must comply. With one male room and one female room, each "use" has only one room. **The conservative read is that both rooms must comply.** Needs confirmation.
  - **202.3 Exception 2:** technical infeasibility allows "maximum extent feasible."
- **Path of travel:** 35.151(b)(4) applies to alterations of a *primary function* area. Restrooms are not primary function areas by themselves. The wrestling room work (W1) could count as primary-function work, and that would bring in the path of travel and the restrooms serving it, subject to the 20% disproportionality rule (35.151(b)(4)(iii)).

SOURCE: S1 (28 CFR 35.151(b)(1), (b)(4), (c)(3); 2010 Standards 202.3, 202.4, 213.2, 213.2.1); S3.

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards (DOJ, eff. March 15, 2012); 28 CFR 35.151; Alabama DCM adoption April 18, 2011.

CONFIDENCE: HIGH that 2010 ADA/603 governs the new restrooms. MEDIUM on how Exception 4 and path of travel apply here.

## R-003.2 — Technical minimums for a single-user toilet room

QUESTION: What are the 2010 ADA Standards minimums for a single-user toilet room: turning space, water closet, grab bars, lavatory, door, mirror, accessories, signage?

ANSWER (one paragraph): The room needs:
- **Turning space:** a 60 in circle or a T-shape in a 60 in square (304.3, 603.2.1).
- **Water closet:** clearance of 60 in from the side wall by 56 in from the rear wall (604.3.1). Centerline 16–18 in from the side wall (604.2). Seat height 17–19 in to the top of the seat (604.4).
- **Grab bars:** side bar 42 in long minimum, starting 12 in max from the rear wall and extending to 54 in min from it (604.5.1). Rear bar 36 in min, extending 12 in to one side of the centerline and 24 in to the other (604.5.2). Both mounted horizontally, 33–36 in to the top of the gripping surface (609.4).
- **Lavatory:** rim 34 in max (606.3), forward approach with knee and toe clearance (606.2, 305, 306), insulated pipes (606.5).
- **Door:** 32 in min clear width (404.2.3), maneuvering clearances per Table 404.2.4.1, hardware 34–48 in AFF with no tight grasping (404.2.7, 309.4), 5 lbf max opening force (404.2.9).
- **Door swing:** the door may not swing into any fixture's clear floor space or clearance, but may swing into the turning space (603.2.3). Exception 2: a door to a room for individual use may swing over a fixture clearance if a 30 × 48 in clear floor space (305.3) is provided inside the room beyond the door swing.
- **Clearance overlaps** are allowed (603.2.2, 604.3.2), but no other fixture may sit inside the water closet clearance (604.3.2).
- **Mirror** over a lavatory: bottom edge of the reflecting surface 40 in max (603.3).
- **Toilet paper dispenser:** 7–9 in in front of the water closet, outlet 15–48 in AFF (604.7). Flush control on the open side (604.6). Coat hooks within reach range, shelves 40–48 in (603.4).
- **Signage:** room ID signs comply with 703.1/703.2/703.5 (216.2). Tactile characters 48–60 in AFF to baseline, on the latch side, with an 18 × 18 in clear floor space beyond the door swing (703.4.1, 703.4.2).
- If the rooms are designated unisex, 213.2.1 requires privacy latches.

SOURCE: S1 (2004 ADAAG chapters as adopted in the 2010 Standards), cross-checked with S2.

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards §§213.2, 213.2.1, 216.2, 304.3, 305.3, 306, 308.2.1, 309.4, 404.2.3, 404.2.4.1, 404.2.5, 404.2.7, 404.2.8.1, 404.2.9, 603.2.1–603.2.3, 603.3, 603.4, 604.2–604.7, 606.2–606.5, 609.2–609.8, 703.4.1, 703.4.2.

CONFIDENCE: HIGH. Read directly from the Access Board's 2010 Standards text and spot-checked against ada.gov.

## What this means for Phase 1 (scope notes; no design decided)

- Each new restroom must fit, at minimum: the 60 × 56 in water closet clearance, a lavatory with a 30 × 48 in forward-approach space outside that clearance, and a 60 in turning space (overlaps allowed). The door may not swing over fixture clearances unless Exception 2's 30 × 48 in space is met. **Room size can't be judged until the walk-through room is measured (D-001).** KEYSTONE will compute fit in `calcs.py` at P1-A-102, not guess it now.
- Plan for blocking in the walls for grab bars (250 lbf, 609.8), a 32 in clear door, and lever-type hardware.
- Path of travel to the restrooms (35.151(b)(4)) may become part of the scope review. The approval path (R-002) should ask the district.
