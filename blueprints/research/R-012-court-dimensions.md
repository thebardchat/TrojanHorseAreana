## R-012.1 — High school basketball court size and runout

QUESTION: What court size and clear space does NFHS give?

ANSWER (one paragraph): NFHS Basketball Rule 1-1: "The playing court shall be a rectangular surface free from obstructions and with dimensions not greater than 94 feet in length by 50 feet in width. IDEAL MEASUREMENTS ARE: High School Age – **84 by 50 feet**." Rule 1-2-1: "There shall be at least **3 feet (and preferably 10 feet)** of unobstructed space outside boundaries." (S1; S2 agrees.) With the preferred 10 ft: (84 + 20) × (50 + 20) = **104 × 70 ft = 7,280 SF**; with the 3 ft minimum: 90 × 56 ft = 5,040 SF (arithmetic). Table 1-1 of the same book recommends planning "a court with ideal measurements … ample out-of-bounds area and necessary seating space."
