| Band | $/SF | Reasoning (ASSUMED, anchored on the CITED numbers above) |

| **LOW** | **$215** | RSMeans localized $200 + ~8 % for seating tier / 2 levels; pre-engineered-style structure, plain finishes, competitive bid |
| **MID** | **$260** | Middle of the localized spectator-grade range ($234–$286, S6); conventional steel + masonry, school-grade finishes |
| **HIGH** | **$340** | Near the top of localized field-house range ($362, S5); upgraded envelope / brand finishes, tight market |

**Say it this way:** *about $39 million all-in (mid), with a credible range of $28–57 million, not counting land. The building alone is about $18–29 million.*

Checks:
- Site share (lines 6 + 7) = 18 % (low), 19 % (mid), 20 % (high) of hard cost; S5 puts site development at 15–25 % of total project cost. The bands sit at or below the middle of that range; a hilly or wet parcel pushes up.
- Portal + Champion Walk together = 2.3–3.5 % of the total ($0.66M–$1.97M). They are not what drives the budget; the building and the parking are.

## NOT included

Land purchase (D-006) · financing / interest during construction · utility tap, impact and connection fees · off-site road, turn-lane or water / sewer extensions (parcel dependent) · escalation to the construction start (Turner BCI is up **5.15 % year over year** at Q2 2026, S3 — add roughly that per year of delay) · sales tax treatment (owner entity, D-007) · donor-program admin (D-044) · trademark work (D-042) · solar / stormwater reuse (D-018) · suites (PARKED).

## Biggest swings (in order)

1. **$/SF band** — each $10 / SF = **$858,000**.
2. **Parking count** — set by zoning / the AHJ (Madison County unincorporated) and shared-parking options. Each 100 spaces ≈ $450k–$680k.
3. **Parcel** — grading, rock, floodplain, utility distance (D-006).
4. **Market timing** — 4–5 % a year.
5. **Portal** — stone type (Indiana full-bed vs cast stone vs precast with limestone face) can swing $0.5M+.
