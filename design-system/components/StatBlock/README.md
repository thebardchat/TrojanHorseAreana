A large brand-colored figure over a small muted caption, used in a centered stat bar under the hero.

**When to use:** two to four headline numbers (capacity, footprint, mats). Put them on a full-width `panel` strip with 1px `border` above and below.

**Rules**
- Figure: `arena-stat` in `brand`. Caption: `arena-label` in `ink-muted`, uppercase.
- Blocks sit in a row with 64px gaps, wrapping; 32px on narrow screens.
- Figures are facts from the Phase 1 spec: 120 x 250 ft footprint, about 800 seats, 5 mats.

**The consumer supplies** the figure and its caption.
